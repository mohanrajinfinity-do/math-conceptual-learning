# Rational Numbers – Diagnostic Assessment

## Purpose

This short diagnostic assessment is designed to identify common misconceptions students may have about rational numbers.

The focus is not only on getting the correct answer, but also on understanding the student's reasoning.

---

## Question 1 – Comparing Fractions

Which is greater?

**1/4 or 1/8?**

### Ask the student:

Explain how you decided.

### What to look for

If the student says **1/8 is greater because 8 is greater than 4**, this may indicate a misconception about the meaning of the denominator.

---

## Question 2 – Equivalent Fractions

Are the following fractions equivalent?

**2/4 and 1/2**

Explain your answer.

### What to look for

Check whether the student understands that equivalent fractions represent the same quantity.

---

## Question 3 – Number Line

Locate the following numbers on a number line:

**1/2, 1/4, 3/4**

### What to look for

Observe whether the student understands the position and relative size of fractions.

---

## Question 4 – Negative Rational Numbers

Which is greater?

**-1/3 or -1/2?**

Explain your reasoning.

### What to look for

A student may compare only the numerical values of 3 and 2 without considering their positions on the number line.

---

## Teacher Notes

The purpose of this assessment is diagnostic rather than evaluative.

Record:

- Student's answer
- Student's explanation
- Strategy used
- Error or misconception observed
- Suggested remediation

Avoid correcting the student immediately. First understand the reasoning behind the answer.

---

## Follow-up

After identifying a misconception, select an appropriate remedial activity and reassess the student using a similar but different question.

The goal is to determine whether the student's conceptual understanding has improved.

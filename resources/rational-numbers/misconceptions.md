# Rational Numbers – Common Misconceptions

## Purpose

This resource helps teachers identify and address common misconceptions students may have when learning rational numbers.

## Misconception 1: A Larger Denominator Means a Larger Fraction

### Common Student Thinking

A student may think:

**1/8 > 1/4**

because 8 is greater than 4.

### Why This Happens

Students may compare the denominator as a whole number without understanding that the denominator tells us how many equal parts the whole has been divided into.

### Diagnostic Question

Which is greater?

**1/4 or 1/8?**

Ask the student to explain their reasoning rather than simply giving the answer.

### Conceptual Explanation

When the same whole is divided into more equal parts, each part becomes smaller.

Therefore:

**1/4 > 1/8**

### Remedial Activity

Draw two rectangles of the same size.

- Divide the first rectangle into 4 equal parts.
- Divide the second rectangle into 8 equal parts.
- Shade one part in each rectangle.
- Ask the student to compare the shaded areas.

### Teacher Observation

Do not focus only on whether the student gives the correct answer.

Ask:

> "How did you decide?"

The student's explanation can reveal the underlying misconception.

### Reflection

Ask the student:

> "What happens to the size of each part when we divide the same whole into more equal parts?"

This helps the student move from procedural comparison toward conceptual understanding.
